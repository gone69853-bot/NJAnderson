Full-Time Result (1X2)

The 1X2 market represents the simplest form of football betting. It covers three possible outcomes at the end of regular time: home win (1), draw (X), or away win (2). This straightforward approach makes it perfect for beginners while still offering value for experienced bettors.

Our prematch football odds for 1X2 markets reflect thorough analysis of team form, head-to-head records, and other relevant factors. We constantly monitor these odds against competitor offerings to ensure competitive pricing.

Double Chance & Draw No Bet

Double chance combines two of the three possible outcomes in a single bet. This market reduces risk by covering multiple outcomes. The three available options are home/draw, draw/away, and home/away. Double chance represents one of the popular total goals, correct score, double chance market trio that forms the backbone of football betting.


Draw no bet provides a safety net by returning your stake if the match ends in a draw. This market effectively removes the draw outcome from the equation. It offers lower odds than straight win bets but provides valuable insurance against draws.


Both markets form important components of risk management in football betting strategies. They allow you to back teams with added security, albeit at reduced odds. Many value bettors use these markets when the straight win odds seem too risky.

Total Goals & Over/Under

Total goals markets let you bet on the combined number of goals scored by both teams. This popular option doesn't require predicting the winner – only how many goals will occur. The standard line sits at 2.5 goals, but we offer alternative lines ranging from 0.5 to 6.5 goals.


The over/under format creates a binary outcome: either more or fewer goals than the specified number. Our fixed odds football betting prices remain competitive across all total goals markets.


Many football betting strategies incorporate total goals markets. They work particularly well when you have strong views on a match's likely character but remain uncertain about the winner. These markets also feature prominently in football accumulator prematch combinations.

Correct Score & HT/FT

Correct score betting challenges you to predict the exact final score. This market offers high odds due to its difficulty but remains extremely popular. We provide competitive prematch football odds across all possible scorelines, typically ranging from 0-0 to 3-3. Correct score markets form part of the essential total goals, correct score, double chance trio that many users focus on.

The HT/FT (half time/full time) market requires predicting the result at both half-time and full-time. With nine possible combinations, this complex bet offers attractive odds. It tests your understanding of how teams typically start and finish matches.


Both markets reward detailed knowledge of team scoring patterns and defensive capabilities. The high odds make them particularly appealing for those seeking substantial returns from modest stakes.
